Privacy policy

Lumio · effective 21 November 2026
LumioSupportTürkçe

In short: Lumio has no accounts and does not collect your name, email or contacts. Your progress and settings stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier and similar data to show and measure ads. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. You can limit ad personalisation at any time.

Who we are

Lumio is made by Taptonic (an individual developer). Contact: atakan.dnmz034@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, settings, your hint count and whether you own Premium are saved in a file on your device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought Premium, Lumio shows ads between some levels and offers optional short videos for extra hints. The ads come from Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads, Google may collect: your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, when permitted), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash/diagnostic data. Google's use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are requested you are asked for consent through Google's consent tool. You can change your choice any time in Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised with your IDFA. You can change this in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Google settings.

Purchases

Premium is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. The in-app purchase library (Unity In-App Purchasing) checks your purchase with the store so it can be restored.

Children

Lumio is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete; your on-device data is removed when you delete the app. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the controls above or Google's own tools. If you live in Türkiye, the EU or the UK you have rights under KVKK / GDPR; contact us at the address above with any request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Ayrıntılar: Google'ın politikası.
