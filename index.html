A light-reflection puzzle · by Taptonic

Rotate mirrors, splitters, filters and combiners to guide coloured beams of light into their targets. Colours mix like real light: red and blue make purple. Light every target with exactly its colour to finish a level.

1000 hand-tuned and solver-verified levels across 100 worlds, unlimited undo, no timers and no lives. Plays offline. Available for iPhone, iPad and Android.

100 dünyada, çözücüyle doğrulanmış 1000 bölüm; sınırsız geri al, süre ve can yok. İnternetsiz oynanır. iPhone, iPad ve Android için.
